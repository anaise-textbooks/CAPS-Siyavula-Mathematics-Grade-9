Multiply a fraction by a whole number

Example:

\(8 \times\) \(\frac{3}{5} = 8 \times\) 3 fifths = 24 fifths =
        \(\frac{24}{5}\) = 4 \(\frac{4}{5}\)

Divide a fraction by a whole number

You can divide a fraction by
        converting it to an equivalent fraction with a numerator that
        is a multiple of the divisor.

**Example:**

\(\frac{5}{8} \div 5 = \frac{5}{8} \div 5\) = 10 fifteenths \(\div 5\) = 2
        fifteenths = \(\frac{2}{15}\)

Division by a fraction

When we divide
        by a fraction, we have a very different situation. Think about
        this:

*If you have 40
          pizzas, how many learners can have \(\frac{3}{5}\) a pizza each?*

To find the
        number of fifths in 40 pizzas: \(40 \times 5 = 200\) fifths of a
        pizza.

To find the number of
        3-fifths: \(200 \div 3 = 66\) portions of \(\frac{3}{5}\) pizza and 2 fifths of a pizza left
        over.

Since the portion for
        each learner is 3 fifths, the 2 fifths of a pizza that remains
        is 2 thirds of a portion.

So, to calculate \(40 \div \frac{3}{5}\), we multiplied by **5** and
        divided by **3**, and that gave us 66 and 2 thirds of a
        portion.

In fact, we calculated \(40 \times \frac{5}{3}\).

<Callout type="note" title="Note">
Division is the inverse of
          multiplication.

So, to divide by a
          fraction, you multiply by its inverse.
</Callout>

**Example:**

\(\frac{18}{60} \div \) \(\frac{2}{3} = \frac{18}{60} \times \frac{3}{2} = \frac{54}{120} = \frac{9}{20}\)
