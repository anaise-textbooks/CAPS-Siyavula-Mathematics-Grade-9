The instruction
        \(72 \div 6\) may also be written as \(\frac{72}{6}\).

This notation, which
        looks just like the common fraction notation, is often used to
        indicate division.

Hence, instead of
        \( (10x^2 + 20x - 15) \div 5 \) we may write \( \frac{10x^2 + 20x - 15}{5}\).

Since
        \( (10x^2 + 20x - 15) \div 5 \) is equivalent to
        \( (10x^2 \div 5) + (20x \div 5) - (15 \div 5) \),

\( \frac{10x^2 + 20x - 15}{5}\) is equivalent to
        \( \frac{10x^2}{5} + \frac{20x}{5} - \frac{15}{5} \).
