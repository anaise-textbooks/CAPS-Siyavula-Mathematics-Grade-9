## Products and squares of binomials

**How can we obtain the expanded
          form of **\( {\textbf(x + 2)(x + 3)}\)?

In order to
        expand \((x + 2)(x + 3)\), you can first keep
        \((x + 2)\) it is, and apply the distributive property:

\((x \textbf{+ 2})(x + 3) \\ = (x \textbf{+ 2})x + (x \textbf{+ 2})3 \\
        = x^2 + 2x + 3x + 6 \\ = x^2 + 5x + 6\)

To expand (x -
        y)(x + 3y) it can be written as (x
        - y)x + (x - y)3y and the
        two parts can then be expanded.

\((x - y)(x + 3y) \\ = (x - y)x + (x - y)3y \\ = x^2 - xy + 3xy - 3y^2 \\
        = x^2 + 2xy - 3y^2\)
